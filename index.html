The Event

A Unique Rugby Match

For 120 years, the Crunch has embodied the most legendary rugby rivalry between France and England, a historic clash that spans generations. Leading rugby creator on social media, Diego Sarthou (djaysonkaravane) drew inspiration from this heritage to create the Crunch Creator.

A unique match bringing together content creators, rugby legends, and former professional players for what promises to be a memorable show.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Crunch Creator takes place on Saturday, May 23, 2026 at 9:00 PM at the Stade Atlantique Bordeaux Métropole in Bordeaux, France.

20 players per team. In total, 32 creators, 8 legends, and former professional players will take part across both squads.

The match is played in an intense, fast-paced format with three 20-minute periods, making it accessible for both players and spectators.

The event will be streamed live on Twitch and the CANAL+ App. You can also attend in person at the Stade Atlantique.

The Crunch Creator was created by Diego Sarthou (@djaysonkaravane), the leading rugby content creator on social media, drawing inspiration from the historic Crunch rivalry.
